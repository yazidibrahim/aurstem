THE PACKAGING ENVIRONMENT

A living product.
A balanced system.

The relationship between produce, material and environment shapes freshness. Our approach considers all three, with application-specific material selection at its core.

Performance depends on the produce, conditions and packaging system. Each application requires evaluation.

01

Modified atmosphere

Packaging designed to manage the environment around fresh produce. Material behaviour and produce respiration are considered together.

02

Breathability

Controlled material behaviour supports appropriate gas exchange for the particular produce and its storage conditions.

03

Moisture management

Packaging designed to manage condensation and moisture conditions, while considering the moisture needs of the product.

04

Gas exchange

Material structures engineered around produce respiration. Oxygen and carbon dioxide transfer are part of a balanced packaging system.

05

Material engineering

Selection and engineering of materials according to application requirements, from film structure to packing-line compatibility.

06

Shelf-life optimization

Packaging designed as part of a wider freshness-management system, alongside handling, temperature and storage.

07

Sustainable material selection

Material choices evaluated for performance, efficiency and sustainability in the context of their intended application.
